Recipe 01 · no cooking

Moong Sprouts Salad

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sprouted moong
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, finely chopped
  • 1 cucumber, chopped
  • 1 green chili, chopped (optional)
  • 2 tbsp coriander leaves
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Salt and black pepper (or chaat masala) to taste

Method

  1. Mix all the ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Squeeze lemon juice over the salad.
  3. Toss well and serve immediately.
Recipe 02 · 10 minutes

Stir-Fried Moong Sprouts

Ingredients

  • 2 cups moong sprouts
  • 1 tsp oil
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves
  • Lemon juice

Method

  1. Heat oil and add mustard and cumin seeds.
  2. Add green chili and turmeric.
  3. Add sprouts and sauté for 4–5 minutes.
  4. Add salt, garnish with coriander, and finish with a squeeze of lemon.
Recipe 03 · 25 minutes

Moong Sprouts Curry

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sprouts
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Sauté onion until golden, then add ginger-garlic paste.
  2. Add tomato puree and spices; cook until oil separates.
  3. Add sprouts and 1 to 1½ cups of water.
  4. Simmer for 10–15 minutes until the sprouts are tender.
  5. Garnish with coriander.
Recipe 04 · makes 8–10

Green Moong Sprouts Tikki

Ingredients

  • 1 cup green moong sprouts
  • 1 medium potato, boiled and mashed
  • ¼ cup finely chopped onion
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • ½ tsp ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp coriander leaves
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • 2–3 tbsp rice flour or oats powder
  • 1–2 tsp oil for shallow frying

Method

  1. Steam the moong sprouts for about 5 minutes and allow them to cool.
  2. Grind the sprouts coarsely — don't make them into a smooth paste.
  3. Mix sprouts, mashed potato, onion, chilli, ginger and coriander.
  4. Add the spices, salt and rice flour/oats powder.
  5. Mix well and make small flat tikkis.
  6. Heat a tawa and lightly grease it with oil.
  7. Cook the tikkis on medium heat for 3–4 minutes on each side, until golden and crisp.
  8. Serve hot with mint chutney or curd.
Recipe 05 · 15 minutes

Sprouted Moong Dal Sundal

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sprouted green moong
  • ¼ cup grated fresh coconut
  • 1–2 green chillies, finely chopped
  • 1 dried red chilli
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • ½ tsp urad dal
  • 1 pinch asafoetida (hing)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • A few curry leaves
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • 1–2 tsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Steam 2 cups of sprouted moong for about 5–7 minutes. They should remain slightly firm.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add mustard seeds.
  3. Once they splutter, add urad dal, dried red chilli, green chilli and curry leaves.
  4. Add turmeric and a pinch of hing.
  5. Add the steamed sprouts and salt.
  6. Toss gently and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  7. Switch off the flame and mix in the grated coconut.
  8. Add lemon juice and serve warm.
Recipe 06 · makes 4–5

Sprouted Moong Pesarattu

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sprouted green moong
  • 1 small piece ginger
  • 1–2 green chillies
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • ¼–½ cup water, as needed
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped (optional)
  • Coriander leaves
  • 1–2 tsp oil for cooking

Method

  1. Steam the sprouts for about 5 minutes and let them cool.
  2. Add sprouts, ginger, green chilli, cumin, turmeric and salt to a blender.
  3. Add water little by little and grind into a smooth, slightly thick batter.
  4. Rest the batter for 10–15 minutes.
  5. Heat a tawa and lightly grease it.
  6. Pour one ladle of batter and spread it into a thin circle, like dosa.
  7. Sprinkle chopped onion and coriander on top if desired.
  8. Drizzle a few drops of oil around the edges.
  9. Cook until the bottom turns golden and crisp, then flip and cook briefly on the other side. Serve hot with chutney.
